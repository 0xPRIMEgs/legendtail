# Contributing

Improve a modeling decision, a diagnostic procedure, or the evidence behind it. A longer handbook is not automatically a better handbook.

## Useful contributions

Include the symptom, asset/tool version, smallest relevant example, explanation, proposed correction, and validation. For a visual failure, include comparable before/after views when redistribution is permitted. For a format change, identify the loader version and distinguish observed layout from assumptions.

If a rule fails on a curved surface, skinned character, transparent effect, or different platform, narrow its scope instead of declaring the original rule universal. Closed robot shells, for example, do not imply that every foliage card must be manifold.

## Evidence labels

Use the labels in [the evidence ledger](references/evidence-ledger.md). Report measured facts separately from artistic judgments. Preserve criticism as useful evidence; do not rewrite a later complaint into blanket approval of an entire roster.

## Review checklist

- Does the change identify when the guidance applies and when it does not?
- Can another artist or agent repeat the procedure from the provided information?
- Are triangle counts, units, coordinate spaces, frame rates, and versions explicit?
- Are relative links valid on a case-sensitive filesystem?
- Does a claim about an export refer to inspected output rather than just source settings?
- Are third-party assets and quotations attributed and licensed appropriately?
- Are private paths, account links, and unrelated conversation details absent?

Keep shared instructions in the relevant guide. Use examples to demonstrate them, rather than copying the entire guide into each prompt. Describe substantive behavior changes in CHANGELOG.md.
