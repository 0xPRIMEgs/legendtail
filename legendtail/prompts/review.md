# Prompt: review a model honestly

> Read LEGENDTAIL.md and guides/16-validation.md. Review the supplied exported asset against its references and technical contract. Inspect silhouette and proportions from front, back, both sides, and three-quarter views; inspect UV orientation/seams, material sampling, topology, triangle scope, rig behavior, and relevant animation. Separate artistic judgments from structural errors and untested runtime claims. Rank findings by visible or functional impact. For each finding, identify evidence, likely cause, a concrete correction, and the consequence of leaving it. Do not modify files unless repair is authorized. Do not treat a concept image as proof of geometry or a desktop render as hardware evidence.

If repair is authorized, add: “Fix the highest-priority requested defects, preserve the approved baseline, re-export, and provide comparable before/after views plus regression checks.”

Use [the review report](../templates/review-report.md). If only images are available, explicitly limit the review to visible evidence; do not invent triangle counts or hidden rig details.
