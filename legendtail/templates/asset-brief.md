## Deliverables and acceptance

- Required files: list engine formats, portable model, editable source, textures, and media.
- Required views: name front/back/sides/close-ups and gameplay view.
- Turntable: specify frame count, fps, duration, and animation.
- Runtime target: name the exact loader/build and available test environment.
- Pass conditions: state visible identity, budget, rig, UV, and package requirements.
- Known constraints: record unavailable targets or deliberate approximations.
